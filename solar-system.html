The Solar System consists of the Sun and everything bound to it by gravity — eight planets, hundreds of moons, and countless asteroids and comets. From scorching Mercury to distant Neptune, each world offers a unique landscape, atmosphere, and story. A journey through the Solar System is a journey through extremes: volcanic moons, icy rings, stormy gas giants, and the faint glow of our star holding it all together.
